Reproduce and contribute

Use the same hardware, model, quantization, CUDA, prompts, KV format and GPU conditions. Record warmup, repetitions, throughput, peak VRAM and output correctness. No matched comparison with llama.cpp, Ollama or vLLM has been established by these measurements.

K7 leads decode at all three depths. Separate copy-heavy rename tasks with N-gram 15 averaged 568.1 tok/s (256.7 without), with 4/6 exact outputs in each arm.
