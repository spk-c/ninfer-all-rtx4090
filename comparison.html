NInfer vs llama.cpp, Ollama and vLLM

A framework for fair comparison of local inference engines without fabricated speed claims.

What NInfer is

A native C++/CUDA engine with converted .ninfer artifacts, local APIs and model-specific speculation. It is not a frontend for llama.cpp, Ollama or vLLM.

Fair benchmark controls

ControlRecord
HardwareSame GPU, power, driver and host load
WeightsSame revision, comparable quantization; disclose differences
ContextSame tokenized prompt and generated length
ExecutionSampling, speculation, concurrency, cache and warmup
ResultsTTFT, decode, prefill, memory, quality and failures
RepetitionInterleaved runs, variability and regressions

Matched cross-engine comparisons are pending. No performance winner is claimed.
